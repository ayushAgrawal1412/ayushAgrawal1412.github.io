Privacy policy – NK Payal backup

NK Payal backup is used only by its owner. It asks Google for one permission: to see and change only the files it creates in the owner's Google Drive (drive.file).

It uses that permission solely to upload and read back encrypted backup files of NK Payal's own books. It does not read any other Drive files, does not collect or share personal data, and sends nothing to anyone else.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
